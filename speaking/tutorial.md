# TOEFL Take an Interview · 完整作战手册（三类题型整合版）


## 第一部分：考试怎么考？

| 项目 | 说明 |
|:---|:---|
| 题型 | 连续回答 4 个问题 |
| 每题时间 | 45 秒 |
| 准备时间 | 无 |
| 形式 | 屏幕显示文字 + 播放录音提问 |


## 第二部分：三种题型 · 三种开头（必背！考场 3 秒判断）

| 题型 | 判断标志（看题目里的词） | 开头句（背这句） | 你的任务 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **习惯类** | *Do you... / How often... / What kind of...* | *To be perfectly honest, I'd say I usually...* | 直接回答事实 + 补充细节 |
| **经历类** | *Describe a time... / Tell me about a time... / Can you share an experience...* | ⭐ *Sure. There was a time when I had to...* | 讲一个具体故事（背景→行动→结果） |
| **观点类** | *Do you think... / What's your opinion... / Do you agree... / Some people believe...* | *From where I stand, I'm totally for the idea that...* | 亮明立场 + 论证 |


## 第三部分：三类模板 · 完整答题结构

### 🔵 模板一：习惯类（Q1/Q2 为主）

> **Step 1 开头**：*To be perfectly honest, I'd say I usually...*
> **Step 2 补充细节**：*On a typical day, I do it right after... It takes about...*
> **Step 3 给理由（感性）** ：*Honestly, I do it simply because it makes me feel...*
> **Step 4 收尾**：*So yeah, that's pretty much how it goes.*


### 🟢 模板二：经历类（Q2 为主，偶见于 Q3）

> **Step 1 开头（背景）** ：*Sure. There was a time when I had to [任务/挑战].*
> **Step 2 行动**：*So I decided to [具体做法]. Instead of [另一种选择], I kept trying to [关键动作].*
> **Step 3 结果/感受**：*In the end, I [结果]. Through this experience, I learned that [教训/感悟].*

**注意**：这类题不要给“两个理由”，考官要的是“故事”。


### 🟠 模板三：观点类（Q3/Q4 为主）

> **Step 1 开头（亮观点）** ：*From where I stand, I'm totally for the idea that [填立场].*
> **Step 2 理由 1**：*To start with, the main reason is that...*
> **Step 3 例子**：*Let's say...（填入素材细节）*
> **Step 4 理由 2**：*Another point is that...*
> **Step 5 让步 + 总结**：*However, I admit that... but for me... So I reckon...*

## 第六部分：三类模板实战演示

### 示例 1：习惯类——*How often do you exercise?*

> *"To be perfectly honest, I'd say I usually exercise about three or four times a week. I go running in the morning before class. It takes me about 20 minutes to run 3 kilometers. Honestly, I do it simply because it makes me feel energized for the whole day. So yeah, running has become a pretty important part of my routine."*


### 示例 2：经历类——*Tell me about a time you had to manage multiple tasks.*

> *"Sure. There was a time when I had to prepare for three final exams while finishing a group presentation in the same week. So I decided to make a detailed schedule. Instead of studying randomly, I divided each day into blocks and stuck to it. In the end, I finished everything on time and got good grades. Through this experience, I learned that planning ahead really reduces stress."*


### 示例 3：观点类——*Do you think online learning will replace in-person learning?*

> *"From where I stand, I'm definitely not for the idea that online learning will replace in-person learning. To start with, the main reason is that face-to-face interaction is irreplaceable. Let's say last semester I worked on a group project — we communicated much better in person than online. Another point is that traditional classrooms offer structure and discipline. However, I admit online learning is flexible, but for me, the human connection is more important. So I reckon they will coexist."*


## 第七部分：经历类 · 所有可用句型（补充工具箱）

### 背景开头（选一句背死）：

- ⭐ *Sure. There was a time when I had to...*
- *Last semester, I encountered a situation where...*
- *Once, I was asked to... and it was a valuable experience for me.*

### 行动描述（选一句）：

- *So I decided to...*
- *Instead of..., I kept trying different ways to...*
- *I chose to... directly.*

### 结果/反思（选一句）：

- *In the end, I succeeded in... and felt a great sense of achievement.*
- *Through this experience, I learned that...*
- *It not only helped me... but also brought me a lot of happiness.*
