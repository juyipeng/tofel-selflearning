=== EMAIL ===
You are organizing a university theater production and need to rent costumes for the cast. You want to contact a local costume shop to inquire about their rental options, prices, and availability. You also need to know if they offer fitting services for the actors.

Write an email to the costume shop manager, Ms. Parker. In your email, do the following:

- Explain the purpose of the university theater production.
- Ask about rental options, prices, and availability for costumes.
- Invite them to the production.

Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

=== DISCUSSION ===
Your professor is teaching a class on history. Write a post responding to the professor’s question.

In your response, you should do the following.

- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.

An effective response will contain at least 100 words.

Dr. Diaz

We’ve been discussing the impact of major historical figures on their respective societies. Some historians argue that individuals like political leaders or influential thinkers shape the course of history more than broader social and economical forces. Others believe social and economical events are the primary drivers of historical change. What do you think has a greater impact on shaping history: individual actions or broader social and economic forces? Why?

I believe individuals and their actions have a greater impact on shaping history. Leaders and influential thinkers can inspire movements, create significant changes, and leave a lasting legacy. Their decisions and actions often alter the course of events in profound ways.

In my opinion, broader social and economic events are more significant in shaping history. While individuals can influence events, it is the underlying conditions and trends that drive major changes. These forces affect large groups of people and create the context in which individuals operate.
