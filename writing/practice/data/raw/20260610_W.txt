=== EMAIL ===
You recently attended a cooking class and thoroughly enjoyed the experience. You found the instructor, Ms. Baker, very helpful and want to thank her for the enjoyable class.

Write an email to Ms. Baker. In your email, do the following:

- Mention why you enjoyed the class.
- Request advice and resources to improve your cooking skills.
- Thank her for the class.

Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

=== DISCUSSION ===
Your professor is teaching a class on economics. Write a post responding to the professor’s question.

In your response, you should do the following:

- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.

An effective response will contain at least 100 words.

Dr. Gupta

Next week, we will be discussing the impact of minimum wage laws on the economy. Minimum wage laws are designed to ensure that workers receive fair compensation for their labor. Some argue that raising the minimum wage helps reduce poverty and improve living standards, while others believe it can lead to job losses and increased costs for businesses. What are your thoughts on this issue? Are minimum wage laws good or bad for the economy overall?
