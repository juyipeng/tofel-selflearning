Strict regulations are essential for safeguarding public health and protecting the environment from pollution. Regulations prevent harmful practices, such as pollution or unsafe products. Without oversight, profit-driven decisions may compromise safety and ethics. Regulations also promote long-term economic and social stability by setting standards that protect vulnerable populations and ecosystems from exploitation.

While some regulation is necessary, excessive rules can hinder innovation and slow economic growth. Overregulation may discourage startups and limit creative problem-solving. Businesses need flexibility to adapt and compete globally. A balanced approach, where essential protections exist without stifling progress, is key to fostering both public welfare and economic growth.
