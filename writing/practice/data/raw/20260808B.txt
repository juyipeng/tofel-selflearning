=== EMAIL ===
You are organizing a holiday party for your student club. You need to find a venue that can host the event and provide the necessary facilities. You want to contact a local community center to ask about their availability, pricing, and what facilities they offer.

Write an email to the community center manager, Mr. Khan. In your email, do the following:

- Explain the purpose of the event and the expected number of attendees.
- Ask about the available dates and costs for hosting the event.
- Describe the facilities you’ll need for the event and ask if the center can accommodate your requests.

Write as much as you can and in complete sentences. Use your own words.

=== DISCUSSION ===
Your professor is teaching a class on anthropology. Write a post responding to the professor’s question.

In your response, you should do the following.

- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.

An effective response will contain at least 100 words.

Dr. Gupta

We’re studying how communities benefit when people from different cultural backgrounds live and work together. Some researchers believe that cultural diversity helps communities by bringing new foods, languages, art forms, and business ideas that create economic opportunities and enrich daily life. Others believe that having shared cultural traditions and common languages makes it easier for people to communicate and cooperate effectively. Which factor do you think contributes more to building strong, successful communities?

I believe cultural diversity helps build stronger communities by bringing valuable new resources and opportunities. When people from different backgrounds share their traditional foods, music, and art forms, they create vibrant festivals and businesses that attract visitors and generate income. Additionally, multilingual residents help local businesses expand internationally and serve diverse customer groups more effectively.

I think having shared cultural traditions and common languages makes communities more successful and cohesive. When residents speak the same language and celebrate similar holidays, they can communicate easily, organize community events more efficiently, and work together on local projects. Shared cultural practices help neighbors understand each other’s expectations and build trust quickly.
