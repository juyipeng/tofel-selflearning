=== EMAIL ===
You and your friend Jordan are planning a trip to Europe during your university's summer break. You have done some research and found some interesting destinations and activities that you would like to include in your itinerary. You want to share your ideas with Jordan and get their opinion.

Write an email to Jordan. In your email, do the following:

- Describe the destinations you have researched.
- Explain why you think these destinations would be enjoyable.
- Ask for their opinion on the suggested itinerary and any additional ideas they might have.

Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

=== DISCUSSION ===
Your professor is teaching a class on economics. Write a post responding to the professor’s question.

In your response, you should do the following:

- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.

An effective response will contain at least 100 words.

Dr. Diaz

We’ve been discussing the impact of automation on the labor market. Because it removes or reduces the human element, automation can increase efficiency and reduce costs, but it can also lead to job displacement and changes in employment patterns. Do you think the benefits of automation outweigh the drawbacks? Why or why not?

The benefits of automation outweigh the drawbacks. It can increase efficiency and reduce costs for businesses, leading to economic growth. While it may displace some jobs, it also creates new opportunities in technology and maintenance to compensate for job losses in other sectors.

The drawbacks of automation are significant. Job displacement can lead to unemployment, social issues, economic downturns and a host of other problems. The focus should be on retraining workers and creating new job opportunities to mitigate these negative effects.
